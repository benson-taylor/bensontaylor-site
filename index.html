ABOUT.TXT

    

      
      

        
Benson Taylor is a British composer, record producer, technologist and entrepreneur, working between his studios in Tuscany and Los Angeles, where his orchestral and experimental synthesis backgrounds unite.

        
David Bowie’s publishing company signed Benson in his early twenties and he has since carved out an incredible career writing and producing music. He is an alumnus of the University of California, Los Angeles.

        
Benson’s music is heard in Netflix’s Orange Is the New Black, Suits, Grey’s Anatomy, Ray Donovan, The Big Bang Theory and 13 Reasons Why. He recently scored Paul Leyden’s Chick Fight, an Amazon Original starring Malin Akerman, Bella Thorne and Alec Baldwin. In addition to the score, he also co-wrote and produced original songs for the film with Grammy-nominated Bones UK.

        
In 2018 he wrote the soundtrack for the film adaptation of the notable Martin Amis thriller London Fields, directed by Mathew Cullen and starring Billy Bob Thornton, Amber Heard, Jason Isaacs, Jim Sturgess, Cara Delevingne and Johnny Depp.

        
Benson founded A Remarkable Idea, a sub-label with Universal Music Group, in 2017.

        
He has co-written with and produced artists including Bloc Party, Maxïmo Park, Bat For Lashes, the Hallé Orchestra, The New Mastersounds, Daedelus, Bo Ningen, Alex Maas (The Black Angels), Pulled Apart By Horses, the Los Angeles Philharmonic, Meshell Ndegeocello, Temples’ James Bagshaw, Lower Dens, MOBO-winning Kairos 4tet, Menace Beach, Robot Koch, The Kingdom Choir and Grammy Award-winning Good Charlotte.

        

        
Benson was awarded an honorary doctorate for his services to music and humanitarian causes in 2016.

        
Benson won ‘Best Original Music’ for his first feature film score, Fear of Water, at the Monaco International Film Festival in 2014.
